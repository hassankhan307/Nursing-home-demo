COMPREHENSIVE PROCEDURES

Professional Home Nursing Services

Specialized, hygienic, and responsible nursing assistance provided at your residence in Karachi following strict medical instructions.

IV / IM Injections

Professional assistance with prescribed Intravenous (IV) and Intramuscular (IM) injections according to appropriate physician medical instructions. Administered with sterile equipment and proper technique.

  • Administered according to doctor prescription
  • Strict aseptic safety & hygiene

Medication Nursing Care

Support with medication-related nursing care according to the patient's prescribed treatment plan. Ensures accurate dosages, proper timing, and adherence to healthcare provider recommendations.

  • Structured administration monitoring
  • Patient comfort & compliance support

Drips Supervision

Home nursing assistance for prescribed IV fluid drips with appropriate nursing supervision. Monitoring flow rates, insertion sites, and patient comfort throughout fluid administration.

  • Supervised drip rate management
  • Regular insertion site inspection

Dressing & Stitch Care

Professional wound dressing and post-procedure stitch care at home. Hygienic cleaning, sterile dressing change, and ongoing observation of wound healing progress.

  • Sterile wound cleaning & dressing
  • Post-surgical stitch maintenance

NG Tube Care

Nursing assistance for patients requiring Nasogastric (NG) tube-related care, feeding assistance, and securement maintenance under proper protocols.

  • Safe tube positioning check & care
  • Gentle patient hygiene maintenance

Foley Catheter Care

Home nursing support for patients requiring catheter-related care, drainage bag maintenance, hygiene protocols, and comfortable position adjustments.

  • Hygienic catheter & bag maintenance
  • Infection risk prevention focus
Medical Disclaimer: Services are provided according to appropriate medical instructions and the patient's individual needs. The nursing service does not replace consultation, diagnosis or treatment by a qualified physician.
